# Wearelegend Invoice Tutorial Video Pack

## Goal
Create a clear video tutorial for both admin and payer flows:
1. Admin creates invoice
2. Payer pays invoice on-chain
3. Admin verifies payment in Invoice Management

Recommended length: 2 minutes 30 seconds to 3 minutes
Format: 1920x1080, 30fps, voiceover + screen recording

## Scene-by-scene storyboard (full tutorial)

### Scene 1 - Intro (0:00-0:12)
On-screen action:
- Open homepage.
- Show Wearelegend branding.
- Quick zoom to invoice workflow sections.

Voiceover (Taglish):
"Sa video na ito, ipapakita natin kung paano gamitin ang Wearelegend invoice flow, from creating invoice hanggang on-chain payment at admin verification."

On-screen text:
"Create -> Share -> Pay -> Verify"

### Scene 2 - Admin wallet check (0:12-0:28)
On-screen action:
- Go to Admin Panel > Create Invoice.
- Show wallet connection card.
- Click Connect Wallet.

Voiceover:
"Unang step, admin wallet muna. Kapag connected at authorized admin wallet ka, pwede ka nang gumawa ng on-chain invoice."

On-screen text:
"Admin wallet required"

### Scene 3 - Create invoice form (0:28-1:05)
On-screen action:
- Fill fields in Create Invoice:
  - Invoice Amount (USDT)
  - Expiry Date and Expiry Time
  - Optional Recipient Wallet
  - Optional Notes
- Show Token Amount auto-calculated from live price.

Voiceover:
"Ilagay ang USDT amount, then automatic na lalabas ang equivalent WLG token amount based sa live buy price. Set your expiry date and time, then optional ang recipient wallet at notes."

On-screen text:
"Token amount auto-computed"

### Scene 4 - Generate and copy payment link (1:05-1:25)
On-screen action:
- Click Generate Invoice.
- Wait for success state.
- Show Invoice ID, amount, token amount, expiry, and Invoice URL.
- Click Copy Link.

Voiceover:
"Click Generate Invoice para ma-create on-chain. Kapag successful, makikita mo ang invoice details at payment URL. I-copy ang link at i-send sa buyer."

On-screen text:
"Invoice created on-chain"

### Scene 5 - Payer opens invoice page (1:25-1:45)
On-screen action:
- Open copied pay link (/pay/:invoiceId).
- Show invoice details and countdown timer.
- Show security notice.

Voiceover:
"Sa buyer side, buksan lang ang invoice link. Makikita ang exact amount, token allocation, at remaining time before expiry."

On-screen text:
"Always verify domain and wallet"

### Scene 6 - Connect wallet and pre-checks (1:45-2:00)
On-screen action:
- Connect wallet.
- Show USDT balance and ETH gas balance.
- If needed, show top-up panel.

Voiceover:
"I-connect ang wallet na magbabayad, make sure nasa Ethereum Mainnet, may enough USDT para sa invoice, at may konting ETH for gas fees."

On-screen text:
"Need USDT + ETH gas"

### Scene 7 - Approve then pay (2:00-2:25)
On-screen action:
- Click 1. Approve USDT.
- Confirm in wallet.
- Click 2. Pay Now.
- Show pending state then success state.
- Show transaction hash and Etherscan link.

Voiceover:
"Dalawang transaction ito: una, Approve USDT. Pangalawa, Pay Now. Pag confirmed na, automatic magiging Paid ang invoice at may transaction hash ka for proof."

On-screen text:
"2-step payment: Approve -> Pay"

### Scene 8 - Admin verification (2:25-2:45)
On-screen action:
- Return to Admin > Invoice Management.
- Click Resync All.
- Show paid status, payer wallet, USDT spent, and paid timestamp.

Voiceover:
"Back sa admin panel, punta sa Invoice Management then Resync All para i-verify ang latest blockchain status. Dito makikita kung paid na, sino ang payer, at kailan nabayaran."

On-screen text:
"Status verified on-chain"

### Scene 9 - Outro (2:45-2:55)
On-screen action:
- Quick montage of Create Invoice, Pay Page, and Management table.

Voiceover:
"And that is the complete Wearelegend invoice workflow. Create, share, pay, and verify in one secure on-chain flow."

On-screen text:
"Wearelegend Invoice Flow Complete"

## 60-second short version script

Voiceover:
"Quick guide: Sa Wearelegend, admin creates invoice by entering USDT amount, expiry, and optional notes. Token amount auto-computes based on live price. Click Generate Invoice, then copy the payment link. Buyer opens the link, connects wallet on Ethereum Mainnet, checks USDT and gas balance, then performs two steps: Approve USDT and Pay Now. Kapag confirmed on-chain, makikita agad ang success state at transaction hash. Finally, admin opens Invoice Management and clicks Resync All to confirm paid status, payer wallet, and payment timestamp. That is your secure end-to-end invoice flow."

## Recording checklist
1. Use a clean browser profile and wallet with test-safe funds.
2. Prepare one sample pending invoice before recording.
3. Keep private keys and seed phrase popups hidden.
4. Blur wallet addresses if needed for public marketing videos.
5. Record at 1080p and zoom in on important buttons.
6. Keep cursor movement slow and intentional.
7. Add captions for: Approve USDT, Pay Now, Resync All.

## Quick edit guide
1. Add lower-third labels: Admin View, Buyer View, Verification View.
2. Add soft click SFX only on critical actions.
3. Add chapter cards every major step.
4. Keep background music low so voice is clear.
